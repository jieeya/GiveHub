GET TO KNOW GIVEHUB

A little more sharing. A lot more possibility.

We believe that useful things should not go to waste when someone else could benefit from them. GiveHub is designed to connect available resources with people and communities that need them.

Be a Part of GiveHub → See How It Works

THE PROBLEM

Useful resources are wasted while needs go unmet.

Every day, households, restaurants, businesses and events may have food or useful items they no longer need.

At the same time, families, students, NGOs and other communities may need food, clothes, books or essential equipment.

Finding one another can be difficult when information is scattered across different groups, contacts and platforms.

Resources have value. Let's help them reach the right people.

OUR SOLUTION

One place to connect giving with need.

GiveHub brings the donation process together through a planned digital platform for listing, requesting, matching and tracking resources.

↔

Connect people

Help donors discover suitable recipients instead of relying only on separate messages or informal contacts.

⌕

Find better matches

Consider category, quantity, location, urgency and availability when identifying suitable donation requests.

✓

Track the journey

Provide donation statuses, pickup details and delivery confirmation to make the process easier to follow.

OUR MISSION

Make useful resources easier to share with those who need them.

Our mission is to create an accessible platform where donors, recipients, NGOs and volunteers can coordinate resource sharing more effectively.

We want to encourage responsible giving, reduce avoidable waste and make the journey from donation to delivery easier to understand.
