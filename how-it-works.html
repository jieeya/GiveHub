SMART MATCHING

Not just a match. The right match.

GiveHub is designed to prioritise donations for recipients who are suitable for the item and may need it urgently.

  • ✓
    Category compatibility

    Does the requested resource match the donation?

  • ✓
    Quantity compatibility

    Can the donation meet the recipient's needs?

  • ✓
    Location and distance

    Can the resource reach the recipient practically?

  • ✓
    Urgency and availability

    Does the recipient's need align with the item's deadline?

2

3

4

5

↗

Not every donation needs a volunteer. GiveHub will support collection arrangements and volunteer-assisted deliveries where available.
